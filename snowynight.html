Snowy Night (雪夜雪夜)

Creator: Strawberry Offal & Tomato Soup Studio (草莓内脏番茄汤工作室)

Genre: Romance, training, guide, galgame, visual novel

Status: Finished

Latest Update: 1 September 2023

Snowy Night (雪夜雪夜) is a Chinese language RPG created by Strawberry Offal & Tomato Soup Studio based on the Japanese comics Hetalia: World★Stars.

Game Summary

Ivan transferred to a school in Moscow, so he temporarily lodged at Ilya's place.

Special Setting

The game uses "Rubles" as its in-game currency, but the exchange rate is not the same as real-world Rubles.
