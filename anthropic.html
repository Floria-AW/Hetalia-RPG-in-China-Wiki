Heta Anthropic Reincarnation (黑塔人类纪行) is a Chinese language RPG (Role-playing Game) created by BaiTuWu (白兔巫) based on the Japanese comics Hetalia: World★Stars.

Story Summary

Warning: the following contains spoilers. Click [Expand] on the right to read a section.

[Expand] 0.APPLE

For more detail: 0.APPLE (HetaAR)

China, Russia, England, France, Germany, Japan and Italy were invited by America to come to a villa in the forest. After a wonderful vacation, before everyone left, America proposes a game called "Killer VS Smiler", where the "Killer" has to guess who the "Smiler" is. All but America, the judge for the game, draws a stick determining their role. England draws the "Killer". After everyone drew their stick, one stick was left over and America takes it. England guesses every player, only for America to reveal that he held the "Smiler" stick after all. America gives England an apple as a reward. England eats the apple before his consciousness fades.

[Expand] 1.WISHES

For more detail: 1.WISHES (HetaAR)

[Expand] WISHES-1

England wakes up and finds himself back in the conference room. America proposes a similar game, calling it "Killer VS Smiler 2.0". This time, England draws the Smiler. Italy questions whether there are any other roles. America brushes it off and joins the game, drawing his own wooden stick. Then, the group disperses to walk around the villa. Russia leaves early after the Killer of this game, Japan, eliminates him. After a while, a gunshot sounds across the villa. The group is at a loss as England is found dead in the conference room and Italy missing. Germany and Japan sets off to look for Italy, while America, China and France wait in the conference room.

[Expand] WISHES-2

After Germany and Japan return to the conference room with Italy, the group has to decide if they should stay to investigate the murder or leave the villa. America pushes the decision making to France, who falls under an influence to make a decision. Initially, France proposes that they leave, but a chill stops him from speaking. He ultimately decides to stay, to everyone's agreement. They split into two groups afterwards: Germany, Japan and Italy are to search the first floor for the culprit; America, China and France are to seal the second floor and the rooms that have been checked.

[Expand] WISHES-3

America's group heads to the storeroom to get wooden boards to seal the rooms. France enquires about a world map he found earlier, finding that he can't remember the country above the USA, which had been crossed out on the map. Both America and China fail to recall the country as well. China and France then question America about the game, feeling that something was wrong with it. America claims that nothing that had happened has to do with him.

While Germany's group searches the library, they discuss the peculiarities of the game. It is revealed that Germany and China have a way to communicate through telepathy, suspected to be an influence of the sticks. After the library is confirmed to be empty, America's group seals the room while Germany's group scouts the kitchen and tea room. In the tea room, Germany notices that Italy is bleeding from his ears, and while Germany is distracted, Japan attacks him from behind. China, who is outside the tea room, spits blood all of a sudden and demands France and America to help Germany. Confusingly, America shoots himself, leaving France to rush to Germany's aid alone. France attempts to stop a crazed Japan, but fails, causing his death.

[Expand] WISHES-4

The scene goes back to a time before Germany is attacked. This time, Germany notices Japan's ambush and dodges in time. China alerts France and America to help Germany, but America shoots himself again, leaving France and China to rush to Germany's aid. They manage to tie Japan up, stopping his rampage. While Italy and Japan are unresponsive, Germany, France and China discuss the situation at hand. Germany and China share about their telepathy and their shared physical state, assuming it was because their sticks had the same symbol. After they exchanged the intel they had, Germany attempts to talk to Japan, who manages to wake up and tell them that his crazed state would stop once the Smiler was dead. They deduce that England is the Smiler, so France and China proceed to retrieve England's stick to bring Japan back to normal. France returns to the tea room alone, and Germany finds that he can't hear China in his head anymore. Japan's influence also seemed to worsen after seeing the Smiler's stick.

France rushes out to find China at the staircase landing, dead. This would mean that Germany was dead too, due to their sticks. He then turns to find an influenced Japan coming down the stairs, somehow untied. France assumes that Italy was dead too. France, having taken America's gun before, shoots Japan, leaving him the sole survivor. He exits the villa in shock, processing everything that had happened. France then sees a mysterious vision of a dance ball.

[Expand] 2.HEAD

For more detail: 2.HEAD (HetaAR)

[Expand] HEAD-1

France finds himself back in the conference room for the world meeting. Convinced that he is imagining things, he plays it off initially, but the déjà vu eventually gets too strong for him to ignore. In order to prevent the game from starting, he urges everyone to leave. To his surprise, everyone agrees. The group leaves, with France needing to push America out himself.

[Expand] HEAD-2

To his dismay, France finds himself back at the meeting once again. Again, he convinces everyone to leave before the game starts, but to no avail. France returns to the meeting for the fourth time, finally accepting the fact that he had fallen into a time loop. Realising leaving with everyone alive doesn't end the loop, France participates in the meeting and draws the stick with the hat symbol as he did the first time. He stays in the conference room with England after the rest leave to explore the villa. While France is thinking about the situation, England creeps closer to him and knocks him out.

[Expand] HEAD-3

France regains consciousness to find England dead. He then finds a gun in the fireplace, triggering his memory of Canada's existence. He is thrown into a vision of Canada, who pleas France not to remember him. As France ponders over the significance of the gun and who the culprit might be, a gunshot sounds and he hears the others rush to the conference room.

Some time after the game just started, Italy gets eliminated by Japan and they team up for the game. They run into Russia and eliminate him, and Russia promptly leaves the villa. Italy and Japan goes to the tea room to find China and Germany there. Italy tells Japan he feels unwell, so Japan lets him rest in the room while he continues the game. Japan then eliminates China, Germany and America. China, Germany, America and Japan hears a gunshot and rushes to the conference room to find England dead and France unconscious. They wake France up, and Germany reveals that Italy had died in the tea room. America calls Russia, who claims that England had left the villa with him. America then calls England. Someone picks up the phone, but the group doubts that it really is Arthur. France sees a vision and goes into hysteria, then kills himself.

[Expand] HEAD-4

France finds himself in another loop. This time, he chooses to stay in the conference room again. England falls asleep and France explores the room. All of a sudden, he finds himself being influenced again to rid of the Killer. Once he snaps out of it, he finally decides that he has to understand the game first if he wants to leave the time loop. In the middle of analysis, he notices that England hasn't died at the usual time. Japan, having eliminated everyone else, enters the conference room with America. Japan then eliminates France and England, ending the game. The four of them meet up with China, Germany and Italy at the hall and leave the villa together. France and America lags behind the group, and France confronts the other about the time loop. America acts clueless, then kills France.

[Expand] HEAD-5

Before heading to the world conference, Russia receives a phone call from Belarus, who requests to meet him at 6pm. Thus, after the meeting and the start of the game, Russia asks America if he can leave earlier. America insists on Russia playing the game as intended, so Russia begins his search for the Killer.

France heads to the library after the start of the game and exchanges intel with the chill (or spirit). Everything else happened as it did the first time, other than Russia not picking up America's call. After everyone else was dead, the spirit tells France to call England, giving France more information about the time loop. Through England, the spirit tells France that America has been replaced by someone called "Allen". France tests this theory in the next loop and gets shot dead immediately.

[Expand] 3.ECHO

For more detail: 3.ECHO (HetaAR)

[Expand] ECHO-1

France tries calling America "Allen" again and dodges the shots this time. America shoots himself after a banter with France, and France decides to call it a loop and leave. Before he manages to, he is shot dead too by England. France enters the next loop and draws the hat stick as before, this time intending to make Russia a Cornerstone. He notices that England did not draw his stick last as he always did before, thus messing up what everyone else had drawn.

It is revealed that both Japan and Germany drew the palm stick this loop. The cast finds themself locked in the rooms they were in. When the doors finally unlocked, they find France dead from a fall. America calls on England to kill the presumed culprit — China, and everyone enters a trance-like state, unanimously agreeing to "execute" China. Japan is the only one unaffected and snaps everyone else out of it. Everyone suspects America of murdering France, much to his displeasure. Russia mentions being curious about a magic circle he saw in the kitchen, so they go there to check it out.

[Expand] ECHO-2

No one ends up being able to see the magic circle other than Russia, Italy and Japan. They turn their attention back to searching for the culprit behind France's death. Under the influence of the Killer stick, China attacks Russia, but regains his senses after Russia shoots his leg. China offers for them to tie him up, keeping him under control in case it happens again. They do, and America knocks him out for good measure. Russia, Germany and Italy head to the storeroom to look for a first-aid box to treat Russia and China's injuries; England and America go to the second floor to search for the culprit while Japan watches over China.

As Russia, Germany and Italy searches for medical supplies in the storeroom, England and America enters, killing everyone inside.

In the kitchen, China wakes up to gunshots outside. He unties himself and finds Japan dead. He hides as England and America enter the kitchen. China overhears their incomprehensible conversation, ending with both of them committing suicide. China then checks on everyone's status, finding everyone dead other than a confused Italy. The both of them leave the villa as China struggles with the influence.

[Expand] ECHO-3

In the outside world, Kuro and Luciano have a call about the time loop occurring outside the villa. The both of them have completely replaced their 1P counterparts in the outside world. South Italy refuses to accept this fact, fighting with Luciano and trying to chase him out, but he is outmatched and forced to play along for the time being.

Another loop has started in the villa. France replicates the first loop to the best of his ability and recalls what had happened before he died the previous loop: he had ran into America and a replaced England, both of whom killed him. France rushes to the conference room at the sound of the gunshot again, but this time, England has the gun in his hand, staging a suicide. As Germany and Italy haven't arrived in the conference room, France and Japan sets out to look for them.

[Expand] ECHO-4

France and Japan gets Italy from the storeroom. The three of them find Germany dead in the tearoom, apparently having committed suicide too. Italy passes out shortly after seeing the body. Before they can understand what was happening, America and China enters as well. They decide to move to a guest room on the second floor to let Italy rest and discuss the situation. Japan seems out of sorts while they are discussing the two suicides, so China brings him out for some fresh air. After Italy wakes up, France decides to leave to investigate more, and America insists on going with him.

[Expand] ECHO-5

France and America proceed to investigate the villa and end up in the tearoom with Germany's body. As France inspects Germany's body and deduces that it wasn't a suicide after all with America's help. America then leads France to realise that China had been replaced by his 2P, Wang An, and was behind Germany's death. A gunshot sounds from the second floor, prompting France and America to investigate.

They return to the guest room, finding Italy dead by Japan's hand and Japan killing himself shortly after. China does not deny the murder when France accuses him of it. America and China both try to gain France's trust while putting down the other. France is gravely wounded by China in the process, nearly killing him until America intervenes. Ultimately, deciding he had seen enough, China takes his own life. America finally discovers France's identity as a Cornerstone and offers France to work with him to escape the time loop. America shows France an influence of the Maverick's stick by swapping their sticks — that the Maverick will seek death once the Leader dies, thus ending the current loop.

[Expand] ECHO-6

A new loop starts. France meets America as agreed the previous loop. Despite France's reluctance, they kill the rest in the villa in order to reduce the risk of any of them becoming a Cornerstone. After which, the two head to one of the rooms on the second floor to exchange information. During the discussion, a gunshot sounds from downstairs despite the two being the only remaining one alive. This leads France to deduce that there is a time difference in the conference room. The discussion ends after France shoots Allen rashly, after Alfred prevents him from questioning Allen further.

Trivia/Extra Fact

In the original work, the term used for "Smiler" is "笑面人" — the same name as the French novel L'Homme qui rit (The Man Who Laughs).[1]

In a later QnA, the creator revealed that this was just a coincidence.
